## Variant: Full canvas inspector

### Design stance
A visualization-first instrument: primary answer dominates, with a right inspector for observation data and advanced math.

### Key choices
- Layout: large canvas area, compact quickbar, right inspector.
- Typography: compact numerical inspector.
- Color: scientific neutral with stronger information hierarchy.
- Interaction: inspector accordions and time slider.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: making the visual answer dominant.
- Weak at: right inspector may compete with geometry space on smaller laptops.

### Best for
A polished later version or visualization-heavy mode.
