## Variant: Precision bezel

### Design stance
Treat the Local Sky Path as a compact navigation instrument with a complete fixed 360° degree bezel.

### Key choices
- Layout: Existing card retained; orientation appears inside the current diagram area.
- Scale: 5° minor ticks, 10° intermediate ticks, 30° major ticks, four numeric quarter-turn labels.
- Interaction: The sky rotates beneath a gold fixed sight; the heading slider simulates device movement.
- Guidance: One short turn instruction below the diagram.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Precision, immediate relationship between phone direction and the full horizon, minimal change to the surrounding app.
- Weak at: The full bezel is visually denser than the other variants.

### Best for
- Users who want a clear instrument-like scale and value seeing the complete 360° relationship.
