## Variant: Quiet forward arc

### Design stance
Keep the rotating sky dominant and show degree marks only where the user is actively aiming.

### Key choices
- Layout: Existing Local Sky Path card and chart dimensions remain intact.
- Scale: A restrained ±60° forward arc with 5° ticks and only −30°, 0°, +30° labels.
- Interaction: The entire sky rotates, while the forward sight and arc remain fixed.
- Guidance: Turn instruction and true Moon bearing share one compact line.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Low clutter and rapid outdoor reading.
- Weak at: Does not show the complete 360° scale at once; less instrument-like than the full bezel.

### Best for
- Users who mainly need to know how far left or right to turn.
