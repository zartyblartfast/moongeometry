## Variant: Focused instrument

### Design stance
Temporarily let the orientation instrument dominate the Local Sky Path, particularly on a phone outdoors.

### Key choices
- Layout: Active mode expands the instrument; on mobile it becomes a focused full-height view.
- Scale: Full 360° bezel with 5° ticks and labels every 30°.
- Interaction: Large fixed sight and target reticle make physical alignment conspicuous.
- Guidance: One large instruction above the dial; supporting values remain compact below.

### Trade-offs
- Strong at: Outdoor legibility, touch use, and obvious alignment.
- Weak at: The active transition is more substantial and temporarily hides the normal chart context.

### Best for
- Users actively trying to find the Moon outdoors rather than casually inspecting the diagram.
